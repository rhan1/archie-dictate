- v1.4 — publish prep: standard macOS paths with migration, configurable signing identity and bundle id, generic vocabulary sample, LICENSE.
- v1.5 — mic never wedges: Objective-C exceptions from AVAudioEngine are caught, one teardown for every end path, rebuild → retry → built-in microphone fallback, first-buffer watchdog (red-team: 33 lifecycle scenarios); pill status words ("no mic", "no key", "offline", "timeout", "held", "no speech") with the paste button only when text exists; first chunk 3–4 s and a filler-aware Whisper prompt; incremental cleanup during speech for long dictations; trailing-silence trim against Whisper hallucinations.
- v1.5.1 — accuracy and latency from live runs: cleanup rules for the comma left by a removed filler, corrections across sentences ("Tuesday. - v1.5.2 — Bluetooth headsets return to high-quality audio after every dictation: the audio engine is released on every end path, with an idle guard and `bt_start`/`bt_after3s` diagnostics; `tools/probes/bt-probe.swift` shows the output sample rate live.
- v1.5.3 — the output mute flag can no longer stay on: restores are read back and verified, re-checked for 5 s across Bluetooth profile flips (a headset muted in A2DP could return from hands-free still muted), the crash record is kept until the restore is verified, and `tools/install-launchagent.sh` refuses to relaunch while a mute is pending.
- v1.5.4 — no lost first words on a Bluetooth headset: the built-in microphone records from the first ⌥ tap until the headset delivers its first buffer (`bridge_ms`, `bridge_src` in the timeline); the pill stays WARMING until the headset is live.
- v1.6 — faster start after ⌥⌥: first ⌥ tap pre-starts mic into 1 s ring buffer, keeps audio 150 ms before second tap (`tap2_to_first_kept_buffer_ms` in timeline), pre-starts after held/error; Bluetooth: built-in mic bridge starts reliably (format aligned to mic), stuck bridge abandoned instead of staying allocated; `bt_profile=` on start line and after each dictation; `bt_hfp_foreign=1` names app holding headset mic; Groq quota awareness: rate-limit counts in `groq-quota.json` (counts only); under 10 % daily requests left switch to Apple STT + local Ollama (`fallback=quota`); keep-warm: unmetered `GET /models` every 4 min (up to 2 h idle) warms connection (20 min idle: 597 → 275 ms); one STT connection for app's life; chunk-boundary repair on by default ("uh, the screenshots" heard as "other screenshots" re-transcribed once); keychain: keys read via `/usr/bin/security` (no prompt on rebuild); unreadable keys use Apple STT + local cleanup (`fallback=no_key`); DictateKit for host apps: `tap()`, `onDiagnostic`, `recoverStaleMute()`, public `SystemOutputMute`, WAV injection for unattended tests (`DICTATE_INJECT_WAV`); `tools/bt-proof.sh`: 2-minute self-checking Bluetooth proof. Token-aware cleanup: mid-dictation cleanup prompt cut to the rule set without examples (816 → 374 prompt tokens per call); when the live Groq token budget can't cover mid + final calls, mids are skipped (`incremental_skipped=tpm`) and one final call runs; a cleanup 429 retries once on Groq's gpt-oss-20b (own rate-limit bucket) before local Ollama (`cleanup_model_fallback=groq:gpt-oss-20b`).
- v1.7 — a Bluetooth headset whose voice link drops can no longer cost a dictation: the headset takes over from the built-in microphone only after 150 ms of real (non-zero) audio; if it delivers only digital silence for 1.5 s the built-in carries the whole dictation (`bridge_src=builtin_fallback reason=headset_digital_silence`), a headset that goes silent for over 0.5 s mid-dictation hands back to the built-in (`bridge_switchback=1`), and one that stops delivering for over 1 s (an engine rebuild) hands back too and takes over again when it returns, with no gap or duplicate at any switch; a headset engine that cannot recover no longer fails a dictation the built-in is carrying (`headset_engine_failed=1`); after 0.7 s of digital silence the headset engine is restarted once so macOS re-requests the link (`headset_restart=1 result=signal|still_silent`); the built-in bridge engine is pinned to the built-in microphone after every configuration change and released rather than left on the headset (`bridge_device_check=ok|drift`); after a failed start the headset engine recovers at most once per second, then backs off 600 ms (`config_recovery_backoff_ms`); `tools/bt-proof.sh --log <minutes>` / `--log-window` reads the system log and passes only if every dropped link was re-created within 1.5 s or the built-in carried the dictation; `DICTATE_INJECT_SCENARIO=headset_zeros` runs an injected WAV as the built-in mic beside a silent headset.
- v1.8 — a dead or drifted microphone is detected on a clock and ends the dictation within 2 s instead of failing silently, and the built-in microphone no longer rides on AVAudioEngine: on 10-12 21:33 the AVAudioEngine built-in child was moved onto the headset aggregate mid-dictation, nothing watched it, 0.29 s kept of 34 s, and bt-proof printed PASS (deep dive: `~/.claude/handoffs/dictate-v6-deepdive/deepdive.md`); the built-in mic is now an owned raw AUHAL on the physical built-in device read back every 100 ms (`builtin_backend=auhal`, `bridge_device_check=`); a 100 ms health clock marks a source stale after 600 ms without frames (startup grace 1.0 s built-in / 2.0 s headset) and transport-silent after 1.5 s of exact zeros, switching only to a fresh source (300 ms, 150 ms of signal) in either direction (fallback no longer latches), warning `health=warn reason=…`, and ending the dictation 2.0 s after the last useful audio with the new outcome `error:mic_dead` (pill word "no mic signal", real duration/bytes, `fail reason=mic_dead …`); one Bluetooth recovery episode governs all four restart paths (one restart per 1.5 s window after 200 ms stable format, success = frames within 1 s, `bt_external_busy=1` when another app holds the headset, at most 3 per dictation, 300 ms settle; non-Bluetooth inputs recover at once); PCM delivered in order outside the recorder lock; session notes track `session=<id> event=start|end` with `capture_intervals=`, `speech_sections=`, `builtin_frames_after_fallback=`, plus `fallback_check builtin_fresh_at_fallback=` and `headset_signal_start=1`; bt-proof's new rules (per session: outcome ok or held with text, ≥95 % coverage after 1 s with no gap >600 ms, ≥3 speech sections, every eSCO drop re-created within 1.5 s with headset signal after it or a fallback onto a fresh built-in that kept frames; empty/missing/duplicate → FAIL; RED on the 21:33 window); WAV hatch scenarios builtin_dies / builtin_drifts (`DICTATE_INJECT_SCENARIO=`).
